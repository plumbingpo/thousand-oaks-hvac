We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Safety

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Thousand Oaks HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Thousand Oaks

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Thousand Oaks HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Thousand Oaks and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever the cause, we diagnose it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
