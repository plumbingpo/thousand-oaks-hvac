Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Riverside

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.
