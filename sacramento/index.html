Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Sacramento

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

The age of your equipment matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the worst heat wave of summer.

Small problems don't stay minor when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
